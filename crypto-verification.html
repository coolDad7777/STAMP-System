🔐 How TSCB Prevents Forgery

1. Time-Locked Challenge

challenge = HMAC(masterKey, meetingID || timestamp) epoch = floor(timestamp / 30seconds) validWindow = 30 seconds only

Stamps can only be generated during a specific 30-second window. Challenges rotate continuously, making pre-generation impossible.

2. Spatial Commitment

geohash = encodeGeohash(lat, lng, precision=7) commitment = HMAC(geohash || challenge, meetingKey)

Location is committed to a 153m geohash boundary. Distance validation ensures physical presence.

3. Cryptographic Binding

binding = SHA256(temporal || spatial || meetingID) signature = Ed25519.sign(binding, privateKey)

Temporal and spatial proofs are mathematically linked. Any tampering breaks the binding and invalidates the stamp.
